# AI Engineering

This section explores the engineering decisions involved in building useful, reliable AI-powered software. It focuses on applying AI to real problems and integrating it into systems people can trust and maintain.

## Who this section is for

- Engineers and teams designing or building AI-powered products and services.
- Technical leads evaluating AI approaches, risks and trade-offs.
- Practitioners responsible for testing, deploying or operating AI-enabled features.

## What you'll find here

- Guidance on identifying problems where AI can provide practical value.
- Considerations for selecting models and working with data, prompts and context.
- Approaches to evaluating quality, reliability and safety beyond a successful demo.
- Engineering trade-offs involved in integrating and operating AI capabilities in production.

## How to use these guides

- Start with the problem and its success criteria before choosing an AI technique.
- Treat evaluation, safety and operational needs as part of the design, not as final checks.
- Adapt the examples and recommendations to your system's users, constraints and risk level.

The goal is to help teams make deliberate engineering decisions throughout the lifecycle of AI-powered systems, from initial design through production operation.
