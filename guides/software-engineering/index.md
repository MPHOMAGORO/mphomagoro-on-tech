# Software Engineering

This section focuses on the practical engineering habits that keep software systems reliable, maintainable and understandable.

It includes thinking on:

- software design and delivery patterns;
- testing and validation strategies;
- maintainability and refactoring;
- team workflows and engineering effectiveness;
- building systems that are easier to evolve over time.

The emphasis is on engineering decisions that help teams deliver work with more clarity, lower friction and better long-term quality.
